✨ Key Features

🏦 Bank & PIN

Full bank system with ATM, transfers, transaction history, and bank cards. All economy requires a Bank Card and a 3‑digit PIN. Request yours at the bank counter.

₿ Crypto SWC

Mine SWC (Source World Coins) in the Crypto Lab. Each rack produces 100 SWC every 30 minutes. Repair racks with bank money and start mining.

🌿 Weed System

Plant seeds (/farm), grow up to 20 plants, harvest 100g of weed every 30 minutes. Sell weed at the Cartel or use it for other purposes. Max 20 seeds/plants per player.

🏡 Houses

Own up to 4 custom‑designed houses (400+ objects each). Houses come with 90 days of ownership, interior, and gun lockers. Buy/sell via commands.

🚗 Vehicles

Buy any vehicle from the shop (2.5M each), choose colors, and park them anywhere with /park. Call them via phone (Y) – 4 slots available.

🍔 Hunger & Thirst

Survival system – monitor your hunger and thirst bars. If they hit 0, you lose health. Restore them at restaurants or vending machines (both cost bank money).

📱 Phone

Press Y to open your phone. Use it to call your parked vehicles instantly. Each vehicle is stored in a slot (max 4).

🎨 Customization

Change your skin at the Victim Shop, equip back weapons, and alter your name color permanently via www.changecolor.com (costs $300,000).

📦 Inventory & Menu

Press N to open the main menu. Access your inventory, GPS, settings, and more. Everything you collect (seeds, weed, SWC, hostages, medic kits) stays in your inventory.

👑 Admin/Owner – Full Request

If you find a cool spot in Los Santos and want a house or a door there, contact debwuoldirty. The owner can map a custom house for you on that location. House price is based on the interior complexity.

⚔️ Deathmatch Arena

New custom deathmatch system with personal kill counter, 8 selectable weapons per match, and real-time stats. The first arena is now open at Santa Maria Beach – more arenas coming soon. Perfect for competitive PvP.

🗺️ GPS System

Full in-game GPS with waypoints for every location: bank, crypto lab, weed farm, vehicle shop, modshop, hospital, gas station, taxi, lumberjack, deathmatch arenas, container spots, cartel, and more. Constantly updated whenever new features are added, keeping all players oriented.

🤖 AI Helper

Server now has an AI model as helper for players, integrated by owner debwuoldirty. It helps new players with daily gameplay, answers math challenges, provides info about world sports, YouTube, social media, and more. Just ask!

📦 New Visual Inventory

Inventory system has been completely revamped with a modern, visual interface. Items are now displayed with icons and colors, making it easier to manage your weed, SWC, bank cards, medic kits, and more. Check it out!

🚶‍♂️ NPC Walkers

The server now has 50+ persistent NPCs walking around Los Santos, added by owner debwuoldirty. They are not distance‑based – they are always there, making the world feel alive. More NPCs are being added every day!

🌐 In‑Game Web Pages

Access these websites via the PC in your house. They offer various services:

www.sourceworldbank.com www.ammunation.com www.weed.com www.crypto.com www.changecolor.com www.thedeepsource.com
  • sourceworldbank – Check balance, transaction history.
  • ammunation – Buy any weapon with bank money (large ammo quantities).
  • weed – Buy weed seeds (max 20 total seeds + plants).
  • crypto – Monitor your mining progress remotely.
  • changecolor – Change your name color forever ($300,000).
  • thedeepsource – Exchange hostages (bought with SWC) for money and SWC. Requires a container (buy one at the container spots).

📌 Additional Information

🔫 Gun Lockers

Each house has gun lockers with 10+ slots, storing up to 999 ammo per weapon. Deposit/withdraw weapons directly from your house.

⏳ Persistent Progress

All your progress (mining, weed growth, house ownership, vehicle parking) is saved in a database. Server restarts won't affect your data.

🏆 Rules & Loyalty

This server is made for friends – no bans, no chat rules. However, cheating against friends is discouraged. Respect the community.

🆘 Support

If you lose items or get stuck, contact debwuoldirty (the owner) for assistance. Guns will be restored if lost due to bugs.

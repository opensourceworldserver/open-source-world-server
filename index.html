Key Features

Everything the server currently offers, constantly expanding.

🚶‍♂️ 150+ NPC Walkers

Over 150 persistent NPCs walking around Los Santos, added by owner debwuoldirty. They are not distance-based – always there, making the world feel alive. The owner plans to keep adding more NPCs daily!

📻 Radio Stream System

New radio system with 5 styles: Hip-Hop, Rock, Electro House, Latino and Pop. Each list has 10 manually selectable songs (not random). Excellent real-time radio experience.

🏦 Bank & PIN

Full banking system with ATM, transfers, transaction history and bank cards. All economy requires a Bank Card and a 3-digit PIN. Request yours at the bank counter.

₿ Crypto SWC

Mine SWC (Source World Coins) in the Crypto Lab. Each rack produces 100 SWC every 30 minutes. Repair racks with bank money.

🌿 Weed System

Plant seeds (/farm), grow up to 20 plants, harvest 100g of weed every 30 min. Sell at the Cartel. Max 20 seeds/plants per player.

🏡 Houses

Own up to 4 custom houses (400+ objects each). 90 days of ownership, interior and gun lockers. Buy/sell via commands.

🚗 Vehicles

Buy any vehicle (2.5M each), choose colors, park with /park. Call them via phone (Y key) – 4 slots available.

🍔 Hunger & Thirst

Survival system – watch your hunger and thirst bars. If they hit 0, you lose health. Restore them at restaurants or vending machines.

📱 Phone

Press Y to open your phone. Call your parked vehicles instantly. Each vehicle is stored in a slot (max 4).

🎨 Customization

Change your skin at the Victim Shop, equip back weapons, and change your name color permanently via www.changecolor.com ($300,000).

📦 Inventory & Menu

Press N to open the main menu. Access inventory, GPS, settings and more. Everything you collect (seeds, weed, SWC, hostages, med kits) is saved.

👑 Admin/Owner – Full Request

If you find a cool spot and want a house or door there, contact debwuoldirty. The owner can map a custom house for you in that location.

⚔️ Deathmatch Arena

New DM system with personal kill counter, 8 selectable weapons per match, real-time stats. First arena open at Santa Maria Beach.

🗺️ GPS System

Full GPS with waypoints for every location: bank, crypto lab, farm, vehicle shop, modshop, hospital, gas station, taxi, DM arenas, cartel and more. Constantly updated.

🤖 AI Helper

AI model as helper, integrated by debwuoldirty. Helps new players, answers math challenges, provides sports info, YouTube, social media and more.

📦 Visual Inventory

Inventory completely revamped with a modern visual interface. Icons and colors to manage weed, SWC, cards, med kits and more.

Additional Info

  • ⏳ ProgressYour progress is safely stored in an external MySQL database linked to the server. Nothing gets lost, so when you come back everything will be exactly where you left it.
  • 🔇 MutingNobody gets muted here. You are free to speak your own language, though the server runs in English and will stay that way for practical reasons.
  • 🤝 CommunityA place for friends and good people who just want to have fun somewhere different in the SA-MP world.
  • 🚫 CheatsFriends don't cheat on friends. You get caught cheating once and it's a permanent ban, no second chances.
  • 🏆 RulesThere aren't many chat rules, just be decent to other players. No cheating, no stealing anyone's info, no exploiting bugs for money, and don't be toxic. The owner doesn't demand respect, but a little kindness goes a long way.
  • 🆘 SupportYou can reach the owner anytime through Gmail. Support requests are handled personally, so you will get real answers, not automated replies.
  • 🛡️ Bug recoveryIf something breaks, the owner personally fixes and restores everything that was affected.

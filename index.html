❓ FAQs

  • ❓ Why was Open Source World Server created?
    Because simply between paying real money to have no limits, or being under pay‑to‑win or pay‑to‑customize, with no personal requests or ideas from players in a game from the stone age, debwuoldirty decided to create his own server and share the fun for free with whoever looks for it, as it has always been.
  • ❓ What is the server's purpose?
    debwuoldirty seeks to offer something new to break the routine of current servers in the SA‑MP world, where you have more participation within the server, see and be part of new things in a cleaner ecosystem for the player, with degrees of difficulty and without them, with limits based on common gameplay rules, not limits based on whether you can afford gameplay with fewer limits.
  • ❓ Who is this server for?
    For anyone looking for a different SA‑MP experience, with deep customization, fair rules, and a community that values kindness over toxicity.

Additional Info

Everything you need to know about the server and community.

⏳ Progress

Your progress is safely stored in an external MySQL database linked to the server. Nothing gets lost, so when you come back everything will be exactly where you left it.

🔇 Muting

Nobody gets muted here. You are free to speak your own language, though the server runs in English and will stay that way for practical reasons.

🤝 Community

A place for friends and good people who just want to have fun somewhere different in the SA‑MP world.

🚫 Cheats

Friends don't cheat on friends. You get caught cheating once and it's a permanent ban, no second chances.

🏆 Rules

There aren't many chat rules, just be decent to other players. No cheating, no stealing anyone's info, no exploiting bugs for money, and don't be toxic. The owner doesn't demand respect, but a little kindness goes a long way.

🆘 Support

You can reach the owner anytime through Gmail. Support requests are handled personally, so you will get real answers, not automated replies.

🛡️ Bug recovery

If something breaks, the owner personally fixes and restores everything that was affected.

Key Features

Everything the server currently offers, constantly expanding.

🐐 Goat Sidequest

Find 100 hidden goats scattered around Los Santos and earn Source Points (SP) – a brand new currency! Spend your SP in the /spstore for powerful rewards. Good luck!

🌟 Source Points (SP)

A fresh currency system! Earn SP by completing missions, sidequests and jobs. Spend them at the /spstore to buy heavy weapons like Minigun, RPG and more.

🚑 Paramedic Job

Revive fallen patients at 75 different locations across Los Santos. Use ambulances stationed at hospitals to start the job. Earn XP, SP and cash for each patient saved. The owner plans to add even more spots!

🍔 Restaurants

Now the city has Cluckin' Bell, Burger Shot, Pizza Stack, and Rusty Donuts scattered around Los Santos. Stay fed and hydrated – find them all!

❄️ Cocaine System

Buy cocaine packages from Big Bear (found in restaurants). Cooldown: 45 minutes between purchases. Sell to other players or directly at the Source Market (grocery stores) for profit.

🎒 Back Weapons

Choose from a variety of weapons to display on your back: AK‑47, RPG, Sabres, Hotseeker, Shotguns and many more. You can even change the color of your back weapon with multiple vibrant options!

🔧 Car Mod Shop

Take your ride to the next level! Choose special colors by ID, nitro, stingers, neons, armour and much more. Visit the mod shop to pimp your vehicle.

🚶‍♂️ 150+ NPC Walkers

Over 150 persistent NPCs walking around Los Santos, added by owner debwuoldirty. They are not distance‑based – always there, making the world feel alive. The owner plans to keep adding more NPCs daily!

📻 Radio Stream System

New radio system with 5 styles: Hip‑Hop, Rock, Electro House, Latino and Pop. Each list has 10 manually selectable songs (not random). Excellent real‑time radio experience.

🏦 Bank & PIN

Full banking system with ATM, transfers, transaction history and bank cards. All economy requires a Bank Card and a 3‑digit PIN. Request yours at the bank counter.

₿ Crypto SWC

Mine SWC (Source World Coins) in the Crypto Lab. Each rack produces 100 SWC every 30 minutes. Repair racks with bank money.

🌿 Weed System

Plant seeds (/farm), grow up to 20 plants, harvest 100g of weed every 30 min. Sell at the Cartel. Max 20 seeds/plants per player.

🏡 Houses

Own up to 4 custom houses (400+ objects each). 90 days of ownership, interior and gun lockers. Buy/sell via commands.

🚗 Vehicles

Buy any vehicle (2.5M each), choose colors, park with /park. Call them via phone (Y key) – 4 slots available.

🍔 Hunger & Thirst

Survival system – watch your hunger and thirst bars. If they hit 0, you lose health. Restore them at restaurants or vending machines.

📱 Phone

Press Y to open your phone. Call your parked vehicles instantly. Each vehicle is stored in a slot (max 4).

🎨 Customization

Change your skin at Binco, ZIP, or SubUrban, equip back weapons, and change your name color permanently via www.changecolor.com ($300,000).

📦 Inventory & Menu

Press N to open the main menu. Access inventory, GPS, settings and more. Everything you collect (seeds, weed, SWC, hostages, med kits) is saved.

👑 Admin/Owner – Full Request

If you find a cool spot and want a house or door there, contact debwuoldirty. The owner can map a custom house for you in that location.

⚔️ Deathmatch Arena

New DM system with personal kill counter, 8 selectable weapons per match, real‑time stats. First arena open at Santa Maria Beach.

🗺️ GPS System

Full GPS with waypoints for every location: bank, crypto lab, farm, vehicle shop, modshop, hospital, gas station, taxi, DM arenas, cartel and more. Constantly updated.

🤖 AI Helper

AI model as helper, integrated by debwuoldirty. Helps new players, answers math challenges, provides sports info, YouTube, social media and more.

📦 Visual Inventory

Inventory completely revamped with a modern visual interface. Icons and colors to manage weed, SWC, cards, med kits and more.
